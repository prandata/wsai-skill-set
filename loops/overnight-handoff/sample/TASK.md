# Tidy the TODO list

Tidy `TODO.md` in four steps, in this order. Edit only `TODO.md` (and `HANDOFF.md`).
Do not add, invent or reword tasks beyond what a step says.

1. **One format.** Make every task a line of the form `- [ ] task` (open) or `- [x] task` (done).
   Lines written as `* task`, `TODO: task`, `[] task` or `DONE task` become that form.
2. **No duplicates.** Where two lines are the same task (ignore case and a trailing full stop),
   keep one. If either copy is done, the kept line is done.
3. **Done at the bottom.** Put the open tasks under a `## Open` heading and the done tasks under a
   `## Done` heading below it. Keep the `# TODO` title at the top.
4. **Priority order.** Under `## Open`, order tasks by their tag: `(p1)` first, then `(p2)`, then
   untagged. Keep the existing order within each group.
